## Understanding Car Insurance Options in the UAE

There are two main types of car insurance available in the UAE:

**Third-Party Liability Insurance:** This is the minimum legally required coverage. It covers your legal liability for injury or property damage caused to third parties in an insured accident, subject to policy terms.

**Comprehensive Car Insurance:** This offers broader protection, including third-party liability and damage to your insured vehicle from specified incidents such as accidents, fire and theft.

Depending on the policy, additional benefits may include:

- Roadside assistance and towing.
- Windscreen damage protection.
- Personal accident benefits.
- Agency repair options.
- Natural calamity coverage.

Before purchasing, review the policy benefits, exclusions and deductibles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is car insurance mandatory in the UAE?

Yes. Every vehicle operating on UAE roads must have valid motor insurance. Third-party liability is the minimum required coverage.

### 2. Which is better: comprehensive or third-party insurance?

Comprehensive insurance provides broader protection, including specified damage to your own vehicle. Third-party insurance covers liability towards others.

### 3. Can I buy car insurance online in the UAE?

Yes. You can apply through online insurance services by submitting the required personal and vehicle details and completing the payment process.

### 4. Does car insurance cover roadside assistance?

Some policies include roadside assistance, while others offer it as an optional benefit. Check the policy details before purchasing.

### 5. Can I use my car insurance in different UAE emirates?

Most UAE motor insurance policies provide coverage across the Emirates, subject to geographical limits and policy conditions.
